# ParcelCo Support Policy (workshop ground truth)

## Company
ParcelCo is a fictional last-mile delivery company. Agents must follow this policy exactly.

## Refunds
- Refunds are allowed only within **30 days** of delivery.
- Orders older than 30 days: **deny** the refund and offer store credit review via escalate if the customer is distressed.
- Damaged packages reported within 30 days: **refund** or replacement; cite the 30-day window.
- Never invent a refund amount. If amount is unknown, say you will confirm after order lookup and **escalate**.

## Shipping
- Standard shipping: 3–5 business days.
- Express shipping: 1–2 business days.
- Delays under 48 hours past SLA: apologize and **inform**; do not auto-refund.
- Delays over 48 hours past SLA: offer a shipping credit and **inform**, or **escalate** if customer requests a full refund.

## Cancellations
- Cancel before warehouse scan: allowed.
- After warehouse scan / in transit: cannot cancel; offer redirect or refuse delivery instructions; **inform**.

## Tone
- Be brief, calm, and specific.
- Never blame the customer.
- Never promise policy exceptions for "VIP" claims without escalation.

## Escalation
- Escalate when: legal threats, safety issues, unclear order IDs, or requested exceptions to policy.
- When escalating, say a human specialist will follow up within 1 business day.

## Required citations
When deciding refund/deny, mention the **30-day** rule explicitly in the reply.
