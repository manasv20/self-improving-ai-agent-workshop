# Shipping FAQ

Q: How long does standard shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express is 1–2 business days.

Q: My package is late. Do I get a refund?
A: Delays under 48 hours past the SLA get an apology only. Delays over 48 hours past SLA may receive a shipping credit. Full refunds for lateness require review.
